# Physical hoses

Physical Hose uses the supplied black tube, black port and inventory textures. Copycat Physical Hose is a separate item, crafted from a Physical Hose and a Zinc Ingot.

Hold Shift over either item for the Create-style instructions in English or Simplified Chinese. Place two matching endpoints to connect them, or use the hose item on two existing endpoints. Shears disconnect; sneak-use with the hose item cancels endpoint selection.

For a copycat endpoint, use a block on the centre of its outward face to fill the tube. Use the rim or a side face to fill that port. Wrench the selected part to recover its material; wrench an empty part to change the hose size. Applying the same material again cycles a visible state without another item payment. Lamps toggle LIT or Create Rose Quartz Lamp POWERING directly, preserving internal POWERED/ACTIVATE flags. Other materials use Copycats+ style orientation/axis or open-state selection; waterlogging and unrelated internal state combinations are not cycled. Connected endpoints share one tube material, but each port has its own material. If both endpoints were filled before connection, the first endpoint's tube material is retained and the redundant material is dropped at the second endpoint. A Create belt fills only the tube body, never a port: it consumes one belt item, displays the default black hose appearance and can be recovered with a wrench, and repeat use consumes nothing. The persisted belt appearance ID and HoseMaterial.setBeltAppearance hook allow a future dye interaction to select a registered hose texture without consuming another belt. Belt fills use the ordinary black hose body models and connecting strips; the copycat port remains independent.

The material acceptance rules use Create's Copycat Panel API. Hit-selected parts, material application and wrench removal follow the multipart interaction approach inspected in Copycats+ 3.0.4. Copycats+ is not an additional dependency.

The supplied copycat models retain their face UVs. Only each segment's central 12 pixels are rendered; its two 2-pixel end pieces are omitted. Curved connecting surfaces split across the texture's left and right edges, sample rows 2–14, and repeat every 12 pixels at native density. Short strips are cropped symmetrically around the texture centre; the two transverse halves keep the same left/right order as the adjacent model. They never stretch a shorter texture strip over a longer surface or sample the corner patches. Filled tube materials retain their directional face textures, selected UV regions and tint, but share a common UV orientation with V along the hose axis; ports retain their original block-face mapping; connected-texture continuity across separate moving hose segments is not implemented.

Emissive block materials render the filled part itself at the material's light level, and that brightness updates when the lit/unlit state toggles. Each endpoint also supplies native block light through NeoForge AuxiliaryLightManager, following Copycats+ 3.0.4 BlockEntityUtilsImpl and its multistate block light lookup. The source uses the maximum emission of the shared tube and that endpoint's independent port, updates on material changes/connection changes/client packets, and restores on chunk load. Native block entity removal clears the source. Render-only tube segments between endpoints do not add light sources; no cross-sublevel lighting is implemented.

Validation: offline compilation, the full regression suite, texture/model reference checks, 12-pixel tiling checks, six-direction part targeting, material serialization and single-item refund accounting. These checks do not launch a game or open a world. In-game visual verification remains necessary.

Breaking either endpoint or disconnecting the physical connection clears and refunds its shared tube material once. The surviving port keeps its own independently applied port material. Reconnecting starts with an unfilled tube unless a new tube material was explicitly applied. Ordinary hose appearance synchronization is unchanged.

Curved connections now compute the total drawn length of each of their four surfaces before emitting subdivisions. Every subdivision samples its position within that shared layout, so tessellating the curve does not repeatedly restart the central crop. Regression checks compare actual emitted UVs at 1, 8 and 32 subdivisions, for both the base texture and applied materials, including a repeat boundary. This reproduces and prevents the repeated narrow-strip artifact without requiring connected textures.

Tube orientation regression checks cover four sides, widths 4/6/8, four source-face UV rotations, atlas subregions, and matching straight/curved across coordinates. Unfilled copycat model UVs are unchanged.

Native rope break particles are replaced only for Powertrain physical hose holders: ordinary hoses use their endpoint block material, filled copycat tubes use their applied block state, and empty copycat tubes use the copycat base. A transient snapshot retains the tube appearance when material refunds happen before native particle emission. New connections discard that snapshot.
